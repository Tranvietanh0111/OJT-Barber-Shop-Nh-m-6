| Thiều Tiến Dũng | Authentication | `frontend/src/pages/auth, frontend/src/context/AuthContext.jsx, backend/app/routers/auth.py, backend/app/core/security.py, backend/app/core/deps.py` |
| Vũ Anh Đức | Appointment & Schedule | `frontend/src/pages/booking, backend/app/routers/appointments.py, backend/app/routers/schedules.py` |
| Đỗ Ngọc Dân | Database & Backend Foundation | `backend/app/core (trừ security/deps), backend/app/models, backend/app/schemas, backend/app/utils, backend/migrations, docker-compose.yml` |
